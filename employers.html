Screening Quality

Our Rigorous 4-Tier Screening Protocol

How we ensure only the top 5% of candidate applicants reach your interview panel.

1

Resume & Credential Audit

Verification of educational degrees, career tenure authenticity, job title matches, and gap explanations.

2

Technical Assessment

Domain-specific assessments verifying hands-on coding, accounting rules, engineering design, or sales metrics.

3

Behavioral & Fit Interview

Evaluation of communication skills, problem-solving under tight deadlines, and team collaboration culture.

4

Pre-Offer Benchmarking

Verification of current payslips, notice period feasibility, counter-offer likelihood, and location willingness.
